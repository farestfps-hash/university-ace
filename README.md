# Studymaxxing AI Navigator

Create an advanced, AI-powered global university admissions platform named "Studymaxxing" (with tagline "Join&Acquire") designed for high school students to build a competitive portfolio, receive deep AI evaluations, and navigate international college admissions (inspired by 1609.kz and SaveArch.online UI).

---

### 🎨 1. DESIGN SYSTEM & UI/UX (SaveArch Style)
- **Visual Style**: Modern light theme with soft background patterns, sleek cards, rounded containers, and pill buttons.
- **Primary Colors**: Royal Blue (#2563EB) combined with Bright Lime Green (#84CC16) accents.
- **Navigation**:
  - Brand Logo: "Studymaxxing"
  - Nav Items: Home, AI Evaluator, Portfolio & APs, Admissions Roadmap & Calendar, About Us, Contacts.
  - Auth Action: Sign In / Register button (Profile Avatar dropdown when authenticated).

---

### 🔐 2. AUTHENTICATION & SUPABASE DATABASE
- **Supabase Auth**: Email/Password + Google OAuth.
- **Database Architecture**:
  - `profiles`: User details, GPA, target major, high school name, target countries (Hong Kong, USA, Kazakhstan, Europe).
  - `olympiads_honors`: Array of `{ name, year, level (School/Regional/National/International), placement (1st, 2nd, 3rd, Honorable Mention), subject }`.
  - `extracurriculars`: Array of `{ title, organization, role, years_active, hours_per_week, description, key_impact }`.
  - `ap_exams`: Array of `{ subject (e.g., AP CS A, AP CS Principles, AP Physics), score (1-5), year }`.
  - `ai_evaluations`: Cached Gemini AI analysis results, holistic scores, country-specific probability percentages, and missing profile items.

---

---

### 📋 4. DEEP USER PROFILE BUILDER (Data Input Engine)
Build a granular form capturing every detail required for elite college admissions:
1. **Academic Background**:
   - High School GPA (Unweighted & Weighted on 4.0 / 5.0 scale).
   - Target Major / Field of Study (e.g., Computer Science, Robotics, Finance, Medicine).
   - **AP Exams Module**: Multi-select AP subjects (e.g., AP Computer Science A, AP CS Principles, AP Calculus BC, AP Physics 1/C) with scores (1 to 5) and status (Taken / Planned).
2. **Honors & Olympiads Logger**:
   - Multi-entry list where students input: Competition Name, Year, Competition Level (School, Regional, National, International), Placement/Award achieved, and Subject Area.
3. **Extracurricular Activities & Leadership**:
   - Detailed activity slots (up to 10): Activity Title, Position/Role (e.g., Co-Founder, Lead Coder, Project Manager), Organization Name, Hours/Week, Duration, Detailed Impact & Description.

---

### 🧠 5. AI-POWERED ADMISSIONS EVALUATOR (Gemini Integration in Core Features)
Integrate Gemini AI deep evaluation algorithms into the profile assessment workflow:

1. **AI Country-Specific Evaluation Engine**:
   - **USA (Holistic Evaluation)**: AI analyzes rigor of AP classes, depth of leadership in Extracurriculars, alignment between Major and activities, and unique student narrative.
   - **Hong Kong (Quantitative & Academic Rigor)**: AI checks GPA, AP subject match with target major (e.g., AP CS + AP Math for STEM majors), minimum required scores (e.g., 3-4 APs with 4-5 scores), and English proficiency.
   - **Kazakhstan (State Grants & Top Universities)**: AI evaluates olympiad awards, GPA, and admission chances for top Kazakhstani universities and state grants.
   - **Europe (Prerequisite Matching)**: AI checks AP exam equivalence, GPA thresholds, and course alignment with European university bachelor requirements.

2. **AI Analysis Output Dashboard**:
   - **Holistic Profile Score (0-100%)** generated by Gemini.
   - **Admission Probability Breakdown** for selected target countries (Safety, Match, Reach classifications).
   - **AI Strategic Feedback**: Specific commentary on student's Major fit, Olympiad strengths, and Extracurricular gaps.
   - **Benchmark Comparison**: Visual scatterplot positioning the student against 600+ historical past applicant profiles.

- **AI Roadmap Generator**:

- **Custom Event Management**:
  - Ability for users to add, edit, and delete application deadlines, interview dates, and task reminders.

- **Interface**: Named **"Studymax AI"** with badge: `на базе Gemini`.
